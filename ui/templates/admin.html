Change network parameters

Draft new parameters, check them and sign your vote; they take effect once validators holding > ⅔ of the power have signed the same parameters and their votes are submitted together. Leave a field blank to keep its current value. The timing is coupled: the step timeout must be long enough for gossip to reach every node at the chosen max peers fan-out, and every parameter has bounds, or the node rejects the vote.

⅔ name action

A > ⅔-ratified change to one name, the community's deliberate exception to the rules: set its balance (it can mint or reduce), give it a new key, set or clear its bank of record, freeze or unfreeze it, or create a new name with a key and a balance. Every unfreeze gives a new key. Leave a field blank to keep that part as it is. Votes are collected off the network and submitted together, so the name's holder learns of it only once it can enact. Use with care.
