Change network parameters

Propose a new operating profile, or add your vote to an existing proposal — it takes effect once > ⅔ of validator power agrees. Leave a field blank to keep its current value. The timing is coupled: the step timeout must be long enough for gossip to reach every node at the chosen max peers fan-out, or the node rejects the profile.

Reassign a balance

A > ⅔-ratified override of an account's balance — the community's deliberate exception to ordinary payments (it can mint or reduce). Bound to the balance the account holds now, so a stale certificate can't replay. Use with care.
