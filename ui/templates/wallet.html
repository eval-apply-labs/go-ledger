Description, delegation and addresses

Describe yourself, choose the validator your balance backs as stake, or publish where your name is reached: your public address (a bank's public face, or your website), up to 4 IP addresses (a validator's are where the other validators send it their messages) and up to 2 TLS keys (the SHA-256 of your web server's public key, in hex: the current one and the next). What you publish is public. Leave a field blank to keep it as it is, or write - to clear it.

Load a checkpoint from a file

When your bank's node can't be reached, someone may carry in a bank's checkpoint file (its hdls/checkpoint). Paste it here. The wallet checks it against the banks it trusts, as it checks your bank's node, and refuses a forged one or another network's. It then shows your balance as of that file's height, while your bank's node can't be reached or serves an older state.
